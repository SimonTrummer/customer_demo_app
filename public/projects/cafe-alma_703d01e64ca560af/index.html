Why Alma

Made slowly, served warm.

Everything on our counter is baked, brewed and plated in-house – with flour, milk and fruit from farms we know by name.

Baked at 5 am

Our sourdough rests for 48 hours before it meets the oven. Croissants are laminated by hand, every single morning.

Roasted in Vienna

Single-origin beans from a small roastery around the corner, dialled in fresh each day by our baristas.

Seasonal & local

The menu changes with the market: apricots in July, plums in September, pumpkin when the leaves turn.

Our story

Alma started in 2019 as a bakery window with one oven and a borrowed espresso machine. Today we bake for the whole neighbourhood – but the recipes are still the ones we wrote on a napkin in that first week.

We work with twelve farms and producers within 150 km of Vienna, and what isn’t sold by evening goes to a local food-sharing project.
